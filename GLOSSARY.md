# Helix Tech

The website at helixtech.app. It presents a set of open tools and the homelab where they are put to work.

## Language

**Helix Tech**:
The name of the site and of the whole body of work it presents.
_Avoid_: Helix (on its own), the Helix website

**Helix Tech LLC**:
The company that builds the Tools and publishes the site.
_Avoid_: Helix Tech (when the company is meant)

**Tool**:
One piece of software the site presents with a page of its own, such as Helix Toolbox or Touchdown.
_Avoid_: Project, app, product

**Category**:
The kind of job a Tool does, in a word or two: Linux setup, Recovery, Documentation, TV, Game day.
_Avoid_: Type, kind, section

**Helix Toolbox**:
The Tool for setting up and looking after a Linux machine from one menu in the terminal.
_Avoid_: Helix Forge

**Homelab**:
The home network and self-hosted services behind the Tools. A part of the home page, not a Tool.
_Avoid_: Lab, home server
