Privacy

Nothing to track.

This site and the docs at docs.helixtech.app are static pages. They set no cookies, run no analytics and load nothing from anyone else: no scripts, no fonts, no images, no trackers.

What the sites keep

If you pick a theme with the switch in the header, that choice is kept in your own browser’s storage so the page opens the same way next time. The docs also keep which sidebar sections you opened, for as long as the tab is open. None of it leaves your browser, and clearing site data removes it.

The server writes the usual access log: the requested page, the time, your IP address and your browser’s name. It is used to spot and block abuse, and for a private report of how many visits each page gets. It is not shared with anyone.

The contact form

The contact form is delivered by Web3Forms. What you type (your name, email address, subject and message) goes to them and on to Helix Tech by email, and a copy stays in their dashboard. Their handling of it is set out in the Web3Forms privacy policy. Your message is used to reply to you and for nothing else. If you would rather not use the form, email works the same.

Links elsewhere

The tools, their issues and their releases live on GitHub. Once you follow a link there, GitHub’s own privacy statement applies.
